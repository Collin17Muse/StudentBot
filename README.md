# 🎓 College Student Finance Dashboard

A user-friendly, all-in-one Streamlit app tailored for **college students** to better manage their personal finances. This dashboard helps students monitor stock trends, create budgets, estimate loan payments, track savings goals, and stay up-to-date with financial news and tips.

---

## 📦 Features

- **📈 Stock Tracker**  
  Visualize the performance of student-favorite stocks like AAPL, MSFT, TSLA, and more.

- **🔔 Price Drop Alerts**  
  Set alerts to notify when a stock falls below a defined threshold.

- **💰 Budget Planner**  
  Plan your monthly budget by inputting expenses and visualizing savings.

- **📉 Loan Repayment Estimator**  
  Calculate student loan repayment amounts based on interest rates and terms.

- **🎯 Savings Goal Tracker**  
  Track progress toward your personal savings goals.

- **📰 Financial News Feed**  
  Stay updated with curated RSS financial news articles relevant to students.

- **📘 Tip of the Day**  
  Receive a new financial tip each time you use the app to stay motivated and informed.
